# Rush Forecast direction contract

**Thesis:** make the next kitchen check obvious, then let Dana inspect the arithmetic. Extend Qafla's operations dashboard rather than introduce a separate visual identity.

**First viewport:** Snowy White, live/forecast controls, persistent black replay bar with lime simulation label, three explicit KPI cards. Desktop continues into the six-truck matrix; phones lead with ranked actionable rows.

**Hierarchy:** kitchen and pickup time, projected places left, then evidence. Amber risk, orange full and neutral closed always have text labels. Forecast performance sits below operational decisions.

**Signature interaction:** predict at 19:00, advance to 19:15 then 19:31, reveal the closed slot's outcome beside the earlier forecast. A solid observed line, dashed prediction and shaded range carry different meanings.

**Access:** native buttons/select/details, visible focus, keyboard replay, touch targets, bilingual labels, RTL controls but chronological charts preserved. No animation is necessary to understand change.

**Constraints:** simulated bookings never enter the live database. OpenRouter is explanatory only. Existing navigation, auth, carts, payments and live manager closures stay intact.
